## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and SDKs. We use “cookies” here to cover these tools collectively.

Cookies can be:

- **First-party** — set by the Site  
- **Third-party** — set by partners such as Google (analytics/ads)  
- **Session** — deleted when you close the browser  
- **Persistent** — remain for a set period or until you delete them

## How to control cookies

You can manage cookies in several ways:

### On-site controls
Where we provide a cookie banner or preference center, use it to accept, reject, or customize non-essential cookies.

### Browser settings
Most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Check your browser’s help pages for “cookies” or “privacy.” Blocking necessary cookies may break login or other features.

### Google ad / analytics controls
- Google Ads Settings and related ad personalization tools  
- Browser add-ons / opt-outs for Google Analytics where available  
- Device-level ad preferences (mobile OS settings)  

### Industry opt-outs
In some regions you can use industry tools for interest-based advertising (e.g., NAI / DAA / EDAA-type portals). Availability depends on your location.

Turning off advertising cookies does not mean you will see no ads—ads may still appear, but they may be less relevant.
